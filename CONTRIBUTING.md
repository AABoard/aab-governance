# Contributing to AAB Governance

This repository is for public governance, policy, role, and decision-process documentation. Contributions should make AAB operations more transparent, accountable, reviewable, and consistent with public-interest work.

## Contribution Types

- Policy proposals or corrections.
- Role definition updates.
- Review workflow improvements.
- Standards approval process improvements.
- Conflict-of-interest and independence safeguards.
- Public decision records.
- Public meeting note corrections.

## Do Not Submit

- Private personnel information.
- Private donor or partner documents.
- Legal advice presented as final AAB policy.
- Credentials, keys, or operational secrets.
- Sensitive student, school, or pilot implementation details.

## Governance Proposal Format

A good proposal includes:

- Problem statement.
- Proposed change.
- Affected policy, role, or workflow.
- Risks or tradeoffs.
- Implementation steps.
- Reviewers or working groups affected.

## Pull Request Checklist

- [ ] The affected policy or role is identified.
- [ ] Public-risk considerations are described.
- [ ] The change is written as durable policy, not informal notes.
- [ ] Related decision records are added or updated when needed.
- [ ] No private or sensitive information is included.
