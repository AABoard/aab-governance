# Review and Decision Policy

This policy defines how AAB should handle public proposals, registry review questions, standards process changes, and governance updates in GitHub.

## Decision Types

- Editorial: wording, formatting, or documentation corrections.
- Operational: workflow, repo, review, or publication process changes.
- Data governance: schema, review status, source trace, or registry inclusion policy.
- Standards governance: standards review, public comment, approval, or maintenance process.
- Organizational governance: roles, independence, conflict-of-interest, or public accountability.

## Minimum Review Expectations

- Editorial changes may be approved by one maintainer.
- Operational changes should be reviewed by the affected maintainer group.
- Data governance changes should involve data reviewers and website/data maintainers.
- Standards governance changes should involve standards reviewers.
- Organizational governance changes should be recorded in `decisions/` when material.

## Decision Record Trigger

Create a decision record when a change:

- Changes the source of truth for data or documents.
- Changes public review or approval rules.
- Creates or retires a major contributor role.
- Affects public trust, independence, neutrality, or transparency.
- Creates a precedent likely to be reused.

## Public Safety

Do not publish private data, credentials, confidential partner materials, or sensitive student information in issues, pull requests, or decision records.
